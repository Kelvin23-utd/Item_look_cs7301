# Methods used in the offline baselines

Every method of `analysis/bench_match.py` (Track A) and `analysis/bench_detect.py` (Track B).
Citations were checked on 2026-11-17 against a registry record (Crossref, CVF open access, the
NeurIPS proceedings, the TMLR listing on jmlr.org, or the arXiv abstract page); the record is
linked. Weights were downloaded once from the source named, into `analysis/.bench-cache/`
(`analysis/bench_fetch_weights.py` repeats it and checks the checksums). Parameters: stored
constant elements for `.tflite` files (weights, biases and anchors), trainable tensors for PyTorch.
File: the weight file(s) on disk.

## Software

Python 3.12.12 in `analysis/.venv-bench` (packages pinned in `analysis/requirements-bench.txt`):
mediapipe 1.2.3, ai-edge-litert 2.3.0 (parameter counts of `.tflite` files), OpenCV 5.0.0,
PyTorch 2.15.0, transformers 5.23.0, open_clip_torch 4.2.2, timm 1.0.31, ultralytics 8.4.215 with
its CLIP tokenizer fork (`ultralytics/CLIP` at `7ffa84b3`), LightGlue (`cvg/LightGlue` at `5a650c44`).

## Notes on licences

- Two weight sets are research-only: MobileCLIP2 (Apple) and SuperPoint (Magic Leap). Using them
  in an offline comparison for a course project is within those terms; shipping them in the app
  would not be.
- Ultralytics models (YOLO26, YOLOE) are AGPL-3.0: putting one in the app would require releasing
  the app's source under AGPL-3.0 or buying a licence.
- The MediaPipe pages name no licence for the `.tflite` model files themselves; the upstream
  EfficientDet-Lite checkpoints are Apache-2.0 (see `FindBack/app/src/main/assets/models/MODELS.md`).

## Planned but not used

None of the planned methods was dropped. Of the plan's examples, SigLIP, Grounding DINO and
YOLO-World were not run because the plan asks for one CLIP-family encoder and one or two
open-vocabulary detectors; MobileCLIP2, YOLOE and OWLv2 were chosen.
