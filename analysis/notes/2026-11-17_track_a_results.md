## Results

AUC: area under the ROC curve (1 = perfect, 0.5 = chance). EER: equal error rate (lower is better). TAR@5%: share of same pairs accepted at a threshold that accepts at most 5% of different pairs. ΔAUC: the method's AUC minus ours, with a paired interval. A value marked * rests on fewer than 10 pairs of one class; TAR@5% needs 20 different pairs; an interval needs 4 items.
