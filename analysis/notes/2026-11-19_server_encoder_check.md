# Server-side encoder check on the audited same-object cases

Date: 2026-11-19. Script `analysis/identity_server_check.py` (tests: `analysis/test_identity_server_check.py`), run on this Mac on the newest private pull, which holds every scan so far (not in the repository). Commit at run time `86f107b`; the script itself was not yet committed.

**Question.** For objects moved between scans, the phone's whole-crop embedding (MobileNetV3-small) gave weak evidence (`2026-11-19_smoke_audit.md`, `2026-11-19_second_two_pass_run.md`, `2026-11-19_three_scan_run.md`). Would a stronger image encoder run off the phone decide these cases, and do several real views help over one? This is the first, cheap server-side comparison: image encoders only, no vision-language model.

**Setup** (fixed before any result was seen; nothing was chosen on the moved cases):

- **Cases.** The audited facts are treated as ground truth; everything else is unknown. Three moved objects: the query is the later sighting, the right answer is the earlier group (two groups count for moved 3). Four unmoved objects seen again in the next scan of the third run (scan `0dfd8938` to `fb0bf2d9`) are calibration positives.
  - moved 1: observation `03f9e780-48-1` → entry `e4af587b` in `e4144f49`.
  - moved 2: entry `f9a56c12` in `b6221dcb` → `e98ae991` in `0c3c261f`.
  - moved 3: `8c1233db` in `fb65023c` → `a1be09aa` in `0dfd8938` or `30452d65` in `fb0bf2d9`.
  - unmoved 1 to 4: `30452d65`, `fe39d6b3`, `9983a5b5`, `dd8e68a0` in `fb0bf2d9` → `a1be09aa`, `8d3d92de`, `30452d65`, `9d921407` in `0dfd8938`.
- **Groups and candidates.** A group is the set of observations the app assigned to one entry within one scan, including tentative entries dropped at the scan's end. The candidates are every group of every scan that started before the test scan: 81 to 208 groups. They include the app's mixed entries, duplicate entries and one-observation tentative groups. They also include the 1.5 s scan `66ff4bd7`, which the earlier notes call void (one observation, one group).
- **Encoders.** The phone's logged embedding, used two ways: over every observation, and over only the observations with a retained view (the same crops the other encoders see). Plus the three embedding encoders of Track A that are in the local cache: MobileNetV3-large, DINOv2 ViT-S/14 and the MobileCLIP2-S2 image encoder. They run on the retained view JPEGs. None was missing from the cache. Not run: Track A's keypoint matchers and colour histogram (they are not embedding encoders).
- **Check that the views are the right crops.** The app's own MobileNetV3-small, run offline on each view JPEG, reproduces the embedding the phone logged for that observation: cosine median 0.9943, minimum 0.9675 (n = 334).
- **Scores.** All scores are cosines.
  - One view: the query's best view (largest box area × score) against each candidate's best view.
  - Several views: every query view against every candidate view, reduced to the max and to the median.
  - Ranking uses the max, the rule of `identity_replay.score_matrix`.
  - Rank counts ties against the right answer. Margin = the right score minus the best other group's score.
- **Clear negatives.** Pairs of groups of different entries within one scan, where every observation of one group is more than 1.0 m from every observation of the other. The moved cases' query groups are left out.

## Reading the results

**Rank.** With several views (max) and with one view, every encoder ranks an audited right group first in all three moved cases. That includes the phone's own embedding. The median over view pairs is the exception: it puts moved 3 at rank 2 (phone, every observation) and rank 3 (phone, views), while the three stronger encoders keep it at rank 1.

**Where the right score falls among the clear negatives** (several views, max):

- Above the 99th percentile of the clear negatives: 3 of 3 moved cases for every encoder.
- Above the highest clear negative:
  - phone, views: 0 of 3
  - phone, every observation: 1 of 3
  - MobileNetV3-large: 1 of 3 (moved 2: 0.694 against 0.691)
  - DINOv2 ViT-S/14: 1 of 3
  - MobileCLIP2-S2: 2 of 3
- All four unmoved positives are above the highest clear negative for every encoder.
- The largest share of clear negatives at or above a moved case's right score is 0.010 (DINOv2 ViT-S/14, moved 1; n = 3489).

**Margins** to the best other group do not grow consistently with a stronger encoder. Each encoder has one small margin:

- phone, views: +0.088 (moved 1)
- DINOv2 ViT-S/14: +0.004 (moved 1), against a tentative one-observation group labelled keyboard
- MobileCLIP2-S2: +0.021 (moved 3)
- MobileNetV3-large: its smallest margin is +0.114 (moved 3)

Raw margins cannot be compared across encoders, because their cosine scales differ. The clear negatives' median (max mode) is 0.128 for the phone on views, 0.115 for MobileNetV3-large, 0.247 for DINOv2 and 0.540 for MobileCLIP2-S2. MobileNetV3-large is on about the phone's scale, and it gives larger margins than the phone on views in all three moved cases: +0.187, +0.234, +0.114 against +0.088, +0.163, +0.106.

**Several views against one.**

- Moved 1 cannot test this, because its query and its right group have one view each. Under MobileCLIP2-S2 its margin even falls (+0.164 to +0.131), because other candidate groups do have several views.
- Moved 2 and 3, the four view-based scores: going from one view to several (max) changes no rank. It raises the right score by 0.000 to 0.047 and changes the margin by −0.013 to +0.022.
- The unmoved positives: several views raise the right score by 0.000 to 0.261. Their margins move both ways, from −0.157 to +0.169.
- More observations, not more views: the phone embedding over every observation, against only those with a view, raises the right score in all three moved cases (0.521 to 0.573, 0.641 to 0.756, 0.503 to 0.536). The margin grows for moved 1 and 2 (+0.088 to +0.140, +0.163 to +0.259) but shrinks for moved 3 (+0.106 to +0.035). For moved 3, a bowl group with no view at all (`04111878` in `fb0bf2d9`) becomes the best other group.

**A group that may be the same object.** For moved 3, MobileNetV3-large, DINOv2 and MobileCLIP2-S2 all put `015afb8f` in `03f9e780` right after the two right groups (0.470, 0.760 and 0.927).

- That group is moved 1's query: the first run's moved object, which audit fact 1 ties to `e4af587b`.
- Under MobileCLIP2-S2, `e4af587b` comes next (0.782). The first group after those two scores 0.741.
- For unmoved 1 (the same object before it moved), `015afb8f` is the best other group under every encoder, the phone included.
- Whether the third run's object is the first run's object was not audited. If it is, moved 3's margins are measured against the same object, not a different one.

**Cost of a server step** (per case; numbers from the cost tables):

- **Upload at query time.**
  - One view: 4.3 to 33.6 KB.
  - Every view: 1 to 4 crops, 7.2 to 126.7 KB.

  - One view per group: 74 to 182 crops, 1.2 to 2.9 MB.

## What this does and does not show

On these three moved objects, the phone's embedding is not what fails at ranking: compared view against view, it already ranks the right earlier group first. The stronger encoders keep that rank, also under the median, and with MobileCLIP2-S2 more right scores clear every clear negative (2 of 3). But they do not give consistently larger margins over the best other group. Several real views changed the moved cases very little. This is a description of three moved objects and four unmoved ones in one home, from one detector and one person's scans; it is not a rate.

**Limits of the ground truth and the candidates.**

- The only ground truth is a visual audit of these seven cases. Every other group is unknown, so a "best other group" may show the same object (see `015afb8f` above, and the earlier groups of entry `9d921407` for unmoved 4).
- The candidates carry the app's own mistakes: mixed entries (`a1be09aa`'s majority detector label is bowl, 12 of 19 observations, though it is moved 3's object), duplicates, and one-observation tentative groups.

**Limits of the clear negatives.**

- They are pairs within one scan, and the pairs share groups, so they are not independent.
- Their highest values all come from same-label pairs: two bowl groups in `03f9e780` and two spoon groups in `0dfd8938`. These may be look-alike objects.
- No same-model pair was tested.

**Limits of the method.**

- "Several views" means at most four retained views per view group; moved 3's query has 3 views for 10 observations.
- The encoders are used off the shelf, with no fine-tuning. No vision-language model was run.
- No decision rule (threshold plus margin) was tested end to end; thresholds are only read off the clear negatives.
- Times are Mac CPU times, not a server measurement.
